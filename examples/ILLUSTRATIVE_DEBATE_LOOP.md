## 1. Answer

**Illustrative claim:** “In Romans 9:6–8, Paul distinguishes physical descent from participation in the promise.”

**References for the reader to inspect:** Romans 9:6–8.

**What the application would seek to do:** Check that the proposed claim does not go beyond what the referenced passage can reasonably support. The displayed citation gives the reader a way to judge the interpretation rather than accept an AI assertion unaided.

## 2. Challenge

**Illustrative challenge:** “A serious alternative reading emphasizes Israel's corporate identity and the historical roles of Isaac, Ishmael, Jacob, and Esau, rather than treating every statement as a complete account of individual salvation.”

**References to examine:** Romans 9:6–13; Genesis 25:23; Malachi 1:2–4.

**What the application would seek to do:** Represent the alternative without caricature, ground it in passages its advocates actually discuss, and avoid claiming consensus where significant disagreement exists.

## 3. Response

**References to examine:** Romans 9:14–24.

**What the application would seek to do:** Engage the challenge as stated, return to the primary text, and distinguish textual observations from disputed conclusions.
