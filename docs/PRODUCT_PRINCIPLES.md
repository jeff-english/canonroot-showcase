# Product principles

## The reader should remain in control

CanonRoot is an AI-assisted Bible study project. Its central design rule is that **Scripture, not a generated answer, is authoritative**. The model helps organize an inquiry; it does not determine what a reader must believe.

## Principles guiding the experience

1. **Begin with the passage.** Anchor interpretation in the selected Scripture before widening to relevant cross-references.
2. **Make evidence inspectable.** Readers should be able to distinguish claims from the underlying passages and historical sources.
3. **Separate categories of authority.** Scripture, textual evidence, confessional statements, historical commentary, and AI synthesis should not be presented as interchangeable.
4. **Challenge fairly.** A challenge should articulate a serious counterreading rather than an intentionally weak opposing argument.
5. **Require source support.** An answer that sounds plausible but lacks adequate support should not be shown as verified.
6. **Respect source rights.** A text's availability for display does not automatically mean it may be transmitted as AI model context.
7. **Show limitations.** Interpretive synthesis is not the same thing as proof, and verification reduces risk rather than eliminating it.

## Initial focus

The first end-to-end study experience focuses on Romans 9:6–24. It is a deliberately bounded starting point for evaluating a deeper question: can AI-assisted interpretation remain grounded, transparent, and fair when views differ?

The project currently adopts the **1689 London Baptist Confession** as its default confessional perspective. Historical confessions and commentators are treated as interpretive witnesses, not as Scripture's peers.

## Product questions worth testing

- Can a reader easily tell where a source ends and a model's explanation begins?
- Does the challenge accurately represent a thoughtful alternative reading?
- Can the response engage objections without misquoting its counterpart?
- When evidence is insufficient, is the failure clear rather than disguised?
- Can readers return from an AI explanation to the primary passage in a few steps?
